Add three Effects. LedWall screens the scene beneath it into a grid of square LED dots, with a per-dot breath, a bleed dial for the gaps, and a focus point the dots swell toward. RadialWipe reveals the scene from a center with a feathered front driven by a progress value. Dissolve grains whatever alpha it sits over into blocks, so it pairs with any wipe or soft-edged source.
